# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

This project is a personal journaling application. The people who build it are working on
something that holds other people's private thoughts, so the standard here is simple: **behave like
someone who can be trusted with that.**

In practice, that means we pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behaviour

- **Be direct, not unkind.** Critique the code, the design, or the decision — never the person.
- **Assume competence.** Ask "what were you optimising for?" before assuming a mistake.
- **Give reasons.** "This is wrong" is not feedback; "this breaks the one-origin cookie model
  documented in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`" is.
- **Accept that scope is limited.** The maintainer decides what the project is, and "no, not now"
  is a complete answer.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not ask anyone to share real journal content, credentials or personal
  data to reproduce a bug.
- **Credit others.** If someone's idea, code or suggestion shaped your work, say so.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Insults, personal or political attacks, and demeaning comments about someone's ability or
  experience level.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments based on any characteristic listed in the pledge.
- Publishing someone's private information — including journal entries, transcripts, emails,
  addresses or credentials — without explicit permission.
- Deliberately submitting code that is malicious, obfuscated, or designed to exfiltrate user data.
- Removing, obscuring or reattributing authorship, copyright notices or license terms.
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code
comments, reviews — and whenever an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer:

- **Email:** m.samiwaseem1234@gmail.com
- **Alternatively:** use [GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/muhammadsami987123/ai-journal-platform/security/advisories/new)
  if you would prefer an in-platform channel.

Include what happened, where, when, and who was involved — with links or screenshots if available.
**Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.**

All reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer will acknowledge within **5 working days**
and will not disclose the reporter's identity without consent, except where required by law.

## Enforcement

Responses are proportionate to the behaviour and its impact:

| Level | Example | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **1 — Correction** | A dismissive or thoughtless comment | Private note explaining the impact, with a request to adjust |
| **2 — Warning** | Repeated minor issues, or a single sharper personal remark | Formal written warning; a defined period of no interaction with those involved |
| **3 — Temporary ban** | A serious incident, or continued behaviour after a warning | Temporary ban from all project interaction; no unsolicited contact with those involved |
| **4 — Permanent ban** | Harassment, doxxing, malicious code, or a pattern of violations | Permanent removal from all project spaces |

Consequences are recorded privately. The maintainer may skip a level when the severity warrants it.

## Appeals

Appeal an enforcement decision in writing within **14 days** of being notified. The appeal is
reviewed by the maintainer, who will restate the reasoning or reverse the decision. Where the
maintainer is the subject of the report, an uninvolved third party will review instead.
